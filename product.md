OverSight is an enterprise AI control plane that gives companies continuous, real-time visibility and governance over how their data is used by AI systems. It connects identity, data context, and AI agent activity to answer a critical question modern organizations struggle with: who used what data, through which AI, for what purpose, and was it compliant? Unlike traditional data catalogs or AI observability tools that operate in silos, OverSight actively monitors AI actions as they happen, evaluates them against explicit governance and compliance rules, assigns dynamic trust and risk scores, and automatically detects or blocks violations before damage occurs. Every decision and policy evaluation is recorded immutably, creating evidence-ready audit trails for regulations like GDPR and SOX, enabling organizations to safely scale AI adoption with confidence, accountability, and trust.
